## Meal composer and photo analysis

The meal editor uses one selectable grid for photo recognition, spoken/typed meals, favorites and search. Clear AI results are selected automatically; uncertain foods appear unchecked with To confirm. Only selected items are saved, and selection flags are not included in diary entries. Single-food queries search the catalogue; meal descriptions and voice recordings use AI.

POST /identify accepts the existing mode, mimeType and imageBase64 fields, plus an optional UUID requestId. In meal mode, one Llama 4 Scout request returns up to eight identities with name, searchName, preparation, grams, confidence, requiresConfirmation and estimated kcal, protein, carbs and fat per 100 g. The app uses an exact local catalog match when available; otherwise the estimate is ready to use and the user can adjust grams. Missing portions become 150 g and require confirmation. Product-label mode keeps printed-nutrient extraction and conversions.

The app first matches exact food names and synonyms in its local catalog, respecting raw/cooked state. When no unique local match exists it calls POST /foods-search with source: "usda" (no second inference and no Open Food Facts lookup). USDA candidates are cached on the device for seven days, up to 80 searches. Ambiguous matches require a user choice. Failed nutrient lookup never removes a recognized food: it starts unchecked with candidates, Find nutrition and editable portion nutrient fields. The whole card and checkbox can select or deselect any food, including an unresolved one; embedded controls keep their own behavior. Unknown nutrients appear as dashes in the card macro footer and selected totals. Saving directs the user to complete a selected unresolved food instead of writing zero values. Favorites use two horizontally scrollable rows. Explicit matches preserve the estimated grams. Retries preserve existing edits and do not add the same recognition twice.

Each photo response includes workerVersion, requestId, diagnostics (stage, durationMs, returned and accepted counts), code, mode and foods when available. Partial valid results are retained. Error codes distinguish authentication/configuration, invalid image/request, AI quota, capacity, timeout, model availability, empty/malformed response and incomplete food data. Only explicit Cloudflare error 3036 or its documented allowance message is classified as exhausted quota; other 429 errors are not. The Worker logs only those diagnostic fields, never images, tokens, prompts or full model output. The app shows safe English messages and collapsible Technical details, retains the photo and meal after failure, ignores stale results, and offers explicit retry or settings access.

Images remain capped at 1536 pixels on the longest side, without upscaling, and 4 MB. Food diary titles derive from selected ingredients without an extra AI call; existing custom titles remain and visible titles use at most two lines.

## Simplified weekly workout flow

The app no longer requests program reviews or next-workout coaching. Progress reviews remain available. At End week the selected session count generates and activates the next week directly, without a second preview. The weekly programmer applies history-based progression and justified exercise replacements, budgets 40–70 minutes including warm-up, rests and transitions, and treats skipped work as missing evidence rather than strength loss. Optional superset suggestions are limited to supported antagonist pairs; invalid pairings are discarded while the rest of a valid week is kept. The old Worker routes remain for older clients. Upload cloudflare-worker.mjs to enable the updated weekly rules.

Weekly v8 sends short catalogue references (E1, E2, etc.) and compact recent history to the model. It enforces the exact split names for each selected day count and requires every 3-day Full Body session to include upper push, upper pull and lower-body work. The Worker maps references to the exact allowed catalogue IDs before validating the result. Exact, unique normalized ID/name matches remain accepted for older response shapes; ambiguous names never choose an arbitrary exercise. Unknown extra choices and duplicates are omitted only if at least four available exercises remain, then targets, technique groups and actual duration are validated. Counts of resolved, omitted and duplicate choices appear in diagnostics. The Worker never replaces a failed AI week with a standard plan automatically. Short sessions can gain actual work sets within the five-set cap, but rests are never lengthened just to meet the time floor. No extra inference or automatic retry is added.
