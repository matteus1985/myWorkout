Weekly progression

First, aim for one more rep while staying within the target range. Once every set reaches the top of its range with at least two reps left, the app suggests a small weight increase. Without effort logging, two successful exposures are required.

Aim to finish most work sets with 2–3 reps left. Log your effort optionally. End week prepares the next targets from completed sets; skipped work never triggers an increase.
Using the app offline

After opening the app online once, it can load without a connection. Logging and progress are saved on this iPhone.

If you get a new iPhone, clear Safari website data, or remove the saved app, restore from a backup first.
AI food photo setup

Connect your personal Cloudflare Worker to estimate foods, portions, calories and macros from a photo. Review and correct every estimate before saving. The private code stays on this iPhone and is not included in backups.

AI is optional. Manual food search works offline.

Model: Meta Llama 4 Scout Vision (17B parameters), a vision-language model that identifies visible foods and estimates portions and nutrition. Its calorie and macro estimates are approximate and should be reviewed.

This device: no photo analyses yet. Cloudflare’s Neuron usage is shown in its dashboard.

Photos are sent for analysis when selected. Calories and macros are approximate model estimates. Cloudflare’s free daily quota may change; analysis stops when unavailable and never switches to paid usage automatically.

Show setup steps
  1. In Cloudflare, create a Worker named lift-log-food-ai and open its code editor.
  2. Replace the sample code with worker/src/index.mjs from this app folder, then deploy.
  3. In Worker Settings → Bindings, add a Workers AI binding named AI, then deploy again.
  4. Generate a private code here and copy it. In Worker Settings → Variables and Secrets, add a Secret named APP_TOKEN with that value, then deploy.
  5. Copy the Worker’s workers.dev address into Worker address, paste the same private code, tap Save setup, then Test connection.

Use Cloudflare Workers Free and do not enable paid AI usage. The free plan stops requests after its daily AI allowance; a paid plan can charge beyond its included allowance.
