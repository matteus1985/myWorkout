Weekly progression

First, aim for one more rep while staying within the target range. Once every set reaches the top of its range, the app suggests a small weight increase.

Your incline dumbbell press stays at or below 28 kg per hand. Hip thrust stays at or below 85 kg. Your saved log is never changed by a suggestion until you start the next week.
Using the app offline

After opening the app online once, it can load without a connection. Logging and progress are saved on this iPhone.

If you get a new iPhone, clear Safari website data, or remove the saved app, restore from a backup first.
